## Sorting ceremony artwork

- `sorting-hat.png`: user-supplied artwork, uploaded as `hat.png` and renamed for the site. The original PNG transparency and proportions are preserved. Rendered through `next/image` by `src/components/sorting-hat-artwork.tsx`; replaces the initial generated SVG hat.
- `src/components/house-crest.tsx`: original geometric shield designs using Lucide's MIT-licensed Flame, Bird, Sun, and Leaf symbols (https://lucide.dev/license).
- The login, signup, and ceremony scenes use the supplied hat image and locally constructed house crests, with lightweight CSS animation.
