By Flying Carriage (Patna Airport)

Distance: ~3.5 Kilometers (~10 Mins)

Descend at Jay Prakash Narayan International Airport (PAT). Pre-paid cabs and electric carriages are available directly from the terminal gate. Instruct the driver for BIT Patna Campus near Veterinary College ground.
